# `#rules`

## Message 1 — Scope and conduct

# CloudNow Community Rules

These rules apply to messages, profiles, usernames, forum posts, threads, voice
activity, and direct interactions that originate from this server.

## 1. Be respectful and constructive

Treat members, contributors, maintainers, and staff with respect. Harassment,
personal attacks, discrimination, slurs, threats, stalking, dogpiling, sexual
harassment, and deliberately hostile conduct are prohibited.

Disagreement is welcome. Address ideas and evidence, not the person. Contributor
or staff status does not place anyone above these rules.

## 2. Keep the community appropriate

This is a general-audience technology and gaming community. Do not post sexual
content, graphic violence, shock material, illegal content, or material meant
primarily to offend or disturb.

## 3. Use the right place

Use `#help` for support, `#feature-discussion` for early ideas, and
`#development` for technical discussion. GitHub is the source of truth for
confirmed defects, proposed code changes, reviews, and long-term project work.

Search `#faq`, `#support-resources`, existing help posts, and GitHub issues
before opening a new support post. Staff may move, close, or redirect
conversations to keep channels useful.

## Message 2 — Abuse, accounts, and distribution

## 4. No spam, scams, or deceptive promotion

Do not post repetitive messages, mass mentions, unsolicited advertising,
referral spam, credential-harvesting pages, suspicious downloads,
cryptocurrency scams, fake giveaways, malware, or unrelated recruitment.

Do not impersonate CloudNow maintainers, community staff, contributors, NVIDIA,
Apple, Discord, or another member. Do not claim CloudNow is endorsed or
officially supported by NVIDIA.

## 5. No account trading or service abuse

Do not buy, sell, lend, request, or distribute NVIDIA, GeForce NOW, Apple,
Discord, or other user accounts. Do not help steal or reuse sessions, evade
subscriptions, bypass queues or regional restrictions, evade bans or access
controls, abuse third-party infrastructure, circumvent DRM, or facilitate
piracy.

Responsible protocol, interoperability, networking, and security discussion is
allowed when it does not enable abuse.

## 6. Use transparent software and links

There is no CloudNow App Store release. Current repository-documented
installation routes are TestFlight, a release IPA, or building from source:

https://github.com/owenselles/CloudNow#installation

Forks and modified builds must be identified as unofficial. Unknown
executables, paid repackaged builds, malware, and misleading distribution links
may be removed.

## Message 3 — Credentials, privacy, and support

## 7. Protect credentials and personal information

Never post passwords, recovery details, Apple IDs, account details, OAuth
access or refresh tokens, authentication codes or headers, cookies, login PINs,
private keys, signing certificates, full IP addresses, unredacted logs, or
another person's personal data.

CloudNow's QR-based login is legitimate, but a login QR code is sensitive.
Never post, stream, or send one to another person. Staff will never ask for
credentials, tokens, authentication codes, PINs, or QR codes.

If a secret is exposed, remove it, rotate or revoke it, and notify staff. Staff
may remove exposed material without warning to limit harm.

## 8. Keep support constructive

Community support is voluntary. Explain the problem, answer follow-up
questions, and acknowledge help. Do not demand fixes, repeatedly ping
contributors, pressure anyone for release dates, or treat a discussion as a
commitment.

Use the `#help` template and redact attachments. A useful version identifier is
a release tag, TestFlight build number, or commit SHA. Confirmed defects may be
redirected to GitHub.

NVIDIA account recovery, subscriptions, billing, bans, queues, regional
availability, and service-side issues must go through NVIDIA's support.

## Message 4 — Security and moderation

## 9. Report security issues privately

Do not disclose a suspected vulnerability in a public channel, support post, or
direct message to staff. Use:

https://github.com/owenselles/CloudNow/security/advisories/new

GitHub private vulnerability reporting is the canonical route. Discord may
support internal response coordination, but it is not CloudNow's vulnerability
intake system.

## 10. Respect moderation decisions

Staff may remove content or restrict access to protect the community. Actions
may include a reminder, warning, message removal, timeout, channel restriction,
kick, or ban. Severe conduct may result in immediate removal.

Do not evade moderation through alternate accounts or encourage others to
continue a removed discussion. Appeals may be submitted privately through
`{MODERATION_APPEAL_METHOD}`.

## 11. Follow platform rules and law

Follow Discord's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, and applicable law.

CloudNow is an independent, open-source project for personal use. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by NVIDIA. NVIDIA and GeForce NOW
are trademarks of NVIDIA Corporation.
