# CloudNow Community Rules

By joining this server, you agree to:

1. Treat people respectfully. No harassment, discrimination, threats, hateful
   conduct, stalking, dogpiling, or targeted hostility.
2. Keep content suitable for a general technology and gaming community. No
   sexual content, graphic material, illegal content, or shock content.
3. Do not spam, scam, impersonate others, distribute malware, or post deceptive
   links or misleading builds.
4. Do not trade accounts, request credentials, promote piracy, or help bypass
   subscriptions, queues, bans, regional restrictions, DRM, or access controls.
5. Protect privacy. Never post passwords, authentication codes, tokens,
   cookies, private keys, personal data, unredacted logs, login PINs, or login
   QR codes. CloudNow's QR login is legitimate, but the QR code is sensitive.
6. Follow staff directions, Discord's policies, and the full rules in
   `#rules`.
